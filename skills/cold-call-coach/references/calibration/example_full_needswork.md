TOP 3 IMPROVEMENT AREAS (quote, then prescribe the playbook line)

Use instead: "I know this is a little random, I was just calling about the property on Pine Hollow, wanted to see if you had any plans for it?" 2. They said: "the number we agree upon is the number you're walking away with. No penny less from it. So with that in mind... did you have like a last number or figure you were looking to walk away with?" Use instead: "My partner handles the final numbers and the deeper qualification." Negotiating a walk-away number is closing on call one and breaks the gatherer frame.

Use instead: "It should only take 5 to 10 minutes, we review what the property qualifies for, and at the end all we need is a simple yes or no. Would tomorrow at 10 AM or 2 PM work better?" An either-or specific time with a time box and defined result prevents the ghost that "any weekday after 10 a.m." invites.
