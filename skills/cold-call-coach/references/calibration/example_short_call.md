Uh I was just calling to see if you're interested in selling it." The seller declined politely but firmly, "No, I'm not. Thank you.", Caller B fished once for other properties, got "Nope. Thank you.", and ended with "Uh-huh. Got it. Thank you." The delivery notes show her rushing to squeeze questions in after the no.

ONE FIX: They said "Oh, okay. Do you have any other properties you may be looking to sell." Use instead: "Okay, no worries. Now, is that no now or just no never?" then the other-properties ask, then "That's ok. Hope you have a great day." The now-vs-never split is the one question that turns a polite firm no into a future follow-up date, and the warm exit leaves the door open.
