# Outreach Templates: first contact that gets a callback

These are **starting points**, not a mail-merge. The whole reason this is a model task and
not a script is that a message which lands is *specific and varied*. Personalize every one
from the provider's own directory row, and change the wording between providers so a group
of subs comparing notes never sees the same text twice.

## How to personalize (do this for each top pick)

Pull the hook from the row you already have:

- **The `Why` field** tells you what they're good at or who vouched. Lead with that.
  ("Saw you do a lot of investor work..." / "You came recommended in the local REI group
  for panel work...")
- **Cross-validated** (call-first) providers: mention the referral, because social proof
  cuts both ways. ("A couple people in the group pointed me to you...")
- **"Confirm [area]" rows:** lead with the coverage question. You need that answered before
  anything else. ("Do you cover [secondary area] too?")
- **Low-confidence / "confirm license/insurance" rows:** keep it neutral and ask for the
  COI + license early; don't over-warm a lead you haven't verified.

Keep it: short (2-4 sentences), plainly worded, investor-to-pro (you're a repeat buyer, not
a one-off homeowner), and honest about who you are. No hype, no "Dear Sir."

## Text / first-contact templates (rotate + adapt wording)

**A: recommended provider (warm):**
> Hey [name], this is [your name] with [your company]. We buy and renovate houses around
> [market]. You came recommended in [the local REI group] for [flooring / panel work /
> etc.]. We do a steady volume of rehabs and are lining up subs we can use over and over.
> Are you taking on new investor work right now?

**B: self-promoter you found (cold-ish):**
> Hi [name], saw your posts in [group]. We're [your company], we flip houses in [market]
> and need a reliable [trade] for repeat work. Do you handle investor jobs, and roughly
> what's your availability?

**C: "confirm coverage" provider:**
> Hey [name], [your company] here. We renovate in [primary + secondary area]. Quick one
> before anything else: do you cover [secondary area] as well as [primary area]? If so I'd
> love to talk about steady rehab work.

**D: specialty / one-off (septic, termite, excavation, etc.):**
> Hi [name], [your company], real estate investors in [market]. We run into [septic
> inspections / termite letters / new water-line runs] on our deals and want a go-to. Do
> you do that kind of work for investors, and can you send your license/COI?

## Voicemail (keep it to ~15 seconds)

> Hi [name], this is [your name] with [your company]. We renovate houses around [market]
> and you came recommended for [trade]. We've got steady work and are picking our subs.
> Give me a call back at [number] when you get a sec. Thanks!

## The vetting-call script (what to ask when they pick up)

Once you have someone live, these questions separate a keeper from a headache:

1. "Do you service [secondary area] as well as [primary area]?" *(for the Confirm rows)*
2. "Do you work with investors/flippers, and can you do volume or repeat pricing?"
3. "What's a realistic timeline for a typical [trade] scope, and will you hold a bid in writing?"
4. "Do you pull permits, and is that included?" *(where the trade requires it)*
5. "Can you send a certificate of insurance and your license number?"
6. "Payment terms: do you take draws on completion, or want money up front?"
   *(heavy up-front deposits are a red flag)*

Jot the answers straight into the call sheet's Notes column so the record stays in one place.

## What NOT to do

- Don't send the identical text to ten subs. Vary it; they talk to each other.
- Don't overstate a low-confidence lead to warm it up; you'll waste both sides' time.
- Don't promise volume you don't have. "Steady rehabs" is fine; "50 houses" if it's 5 isn't.
- Don't send anything on the user's behalf from this skill. Draft it; the user sends it.
