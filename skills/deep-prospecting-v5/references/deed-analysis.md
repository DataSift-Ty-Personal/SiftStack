# Deed Analysis for Deep Prospecting

Analyze deeds "as if your life depended on it." The deed chain reveals ownership history, relationships, and red flags that inform decision-maker identification.

## Key Elements to Extract

### From Current Deed
- **Grantee(s)**: Current owner(s) on title
- **Middle initials**: Critical for identity verification
- **Vesting type**: How title is held (sole, joint, trust, etc.)
- **Instrument type**: Type of transfer (see below)
- **Recording date**: When deed was recorded
- **Consideration**: Purchase price (if shown)

### From Deed Chain (Last 3-5 Instruments)
- Previous owners
- Transfer patterns
- Family relationships
- Time between transfers

## Instrument Types and Red Flags

| Instrument | Description | Red Flag Level |
|------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Warranty Deed** | Standard arms-length sale | Low |
| **Special Warranty Deed** | Limited warranty, common in foreclosures | Medium |
| **Quitclaim Deed (QCD)** | No warranty, often family transfers | High |
| **Executor's Deed** | Estate sale, confirms death | High - L3 |
| **Administrator's Deed** | Intestate estate sale | High - L3 |
| **Trust Deed** | Transfer to/from trust | Medium |
| **Contract for Deed** | Installment sale, title not transferred | Very High |
| **Land Contract** | Same as contract for deed | Very High |
| **Deed in Lieu** | Foreclosure alternative | High |
| **Sheriff's Deed** | Tax sale or foreclosure | High |
| **Correction Deed** | Fixes prior deed error | Low |

## Red Flag Patterns

### Contract for Deed / Installment Agreement
- **Issue**: Seller still on title, buyer making payments
- **Decision-maker**: Original seller, NOT current occupant
- **Action**: Verify with title attorney, may be L4 scenario

### Quitclaim Deed Chain
- **Issue**: Often indicates family transfers, divorce, or clouded title
- **Decision-maker**: May need to trace back to original grantor
- **Action**: Review full chain, look for relationships

### Multiple Grantees
- **Issue**: Joint ownership complicates decision-making
- **Decision-maker**: All parties on title, or survivor if JTWROS
- **Action**: Identify relationship, check for death of any party

### Trust Ownership
- **Issue**: Trust owns property, need to find trustee
- **Decision-maker**: Trustee (often same as grantor for living trusts)
- **Action**: Search for trust documents, Secretary of State if business trust

### Estate/Executor Deeds in Chain
- **Issue**: Confirms prior owner deceased
- **Decision-maker**: Current grantee, or their heirs if also deceased
- **Action**: May trigger L3 research if current owner also appears deceased

## Vesting Types

| Vesting | Meaning | On Death |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **Sole ownership** | One person owns | Goes to estate |
| **Joint Tenants (JTWROS)** | Equal shares, right of survivorship | Survivor gets all |
| **Tenants in Common** | Shares may vary, no survivorship | Share goes to estate |
| **Tenants by Entirety** | Married couples, survivorship | Survivor gets all |
| **Community Property** | Married couples (some states) | Varies by state |
| **Trust** | Trust owns property | Per trust terms |

## Relationship Clues in Deeds

Look for these indicators of family relationships:

- **Same last name**: Likely family (parent/child, siblings)
- **"Et ux" / "Et vir"**: And wife / and husband
- **"Et al"**: And others (multiple grantees)
- **Maiden name in parentheses**: Married woman
- **"FKA" / "AKA"**: Formerly known as / Also known as
- **Same address as grantee**: Family transfer, not arms-length
- **$1 or $10 consideration**: Gift or family transfer
- **"Love and affection"**: Family transfer language

## Deed Chain Analysis Steps

1. **Pull current deed** - Identify current owner(s) and vesting
2. **Pull 3-5 prior instruments** - Build ownership timeline
3. **Note instrument types** - Flag non-standard transfers
4. **Identify relationships** - Look for family connections
5. **Check for gaps** - Missing heirs, unreleased liens
6. **Cross-reference names** - Verify against other sources
7. **Document red flags** - Note anything requiring L2+ research
