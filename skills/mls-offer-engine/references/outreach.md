# Working the call list

The engine finds the listings and prices the offers. A person makes the calls.

## Who to call first

1. Motivated listings (score 45 or more) that carry both a cash and a creative
   offer.
2. Cash-only offers, best score first.
3. The Subject-to sheet, closest to owing the list price first. That is where a
   seller cannot sell for cash and walk away with anything, so taking over the
   loan is the offer that works for them.

Use the Agents sheet to batch it. An agent holding several stale listings is
one relationship: one good conversation reaches all of them.

## The first contact

Phone first. The "First text" column is the opener, written to be read or sent
as is:

> Hi Sam, this is Pat, a local investor. I'd like to make an offer on
> 100 Example St. I can do $120,000 cash, as-is, closing in 14 days. Or I can
> pay $200,000 by keeping your seller's current loan in place, paying them
> $8,000 at closing, and covering your commission. Would your seller look at
> either one?

What makes it work:

- Say what you are: a local investor.
- Both numbers in one message, so the agent can take either to the seller.
- One question at the end.
- Agents have to present offers. Most will, when the offer is clear and real.

## Never

- **Never contact the homeowner directly.** The house is listed. The agent is
  the contact.
- **Never claim a lender pre-approval** or financing you do not have.
- **Never send a lowball under half of list.** That is what the Price watch
  sheet is for: wait for the price that makes it reasonable.
- **Never promise to stop a foreclosure** or imply you can.

## Contracts

- Write contracts "and/or assigns", and tell the agent up front that you may
  assign. Wholesaling rules differ by state: check yours with a closing
  attorney before you market any contract.
- **Subject-to:** the loan stays in the seller's name, and the lender can call
  it due on a transfer. Use a closing attorney who does these, and make sure
  the seller understands it in writing.
- In both creative offers the buyer pays the listing agent's commission on
  top. Say so in the first message: it is the line that gets an agent to take
  the offer to the seller.

## Before any number is binding

The numbers are estimates. Confirm with the agent:

- the payoff and the rate on the seller's loan (and whether it is current),
- the rent the house would get,
- the condition (repairs are a flat placeholder per square foot until someone
  reads the photos or walks the house).

## Follow-up

- Re-run the engine weekly. New listings appear, and price cuts move Price
  watch listings into range (the "send when list is at or under" column says
  where).
- Keep a simple tracker: date sent, agent reply, counter, next step.
- An agent who says no today often calls back after the next price cut. Note
  who was open to a creative offer.
