# Job descriptions and posts

Two different documents. The description defines the job for you, for the
interview, and for onboarding. The post is marketing copy whose job is to get
the right people to apply and the wrong people to scroll past.

## The filter that does the most work

Put the daily KPI in the post.

The archetype who wants to make 200 dials a day is rare and knows it. Naming the
number up front means the people who apply have already accepted it, and the
people who would have flinched in week two never applied. This costs you nothing
and removes most of the bad applicants before you ever read a resume.

## Where to post

- **Indeed.** Best volume for data and sales roles. Expect a heavy majority of
  the applications for a data manager role and noticeably fewer for sales.
- **Facebook groups for real estate virtual assistants.** Better fit, lower
  volume, and the applicants usually already understand the industry. Post in
  several; each group has its own rhythm.
- **Referrals from other investors.** Highest quality by a distance. When
  someone posts publicly that they are letting a good person go for reasons
  unrelated to performance, act immediately: that person is usually gone within
  a day.

## Description template

Fill every bracket. If you cannot fill the daily tasks section, you are not
ready to hire for this role.

```
ROLE: [title]
TYPE: Full time, remote
HOURS: [hours] in [US time zone]
PAY: [$X] per month, plus [commission structure]

WHAT THIS ROLE OWNS
[One paragraph. What breaks if this seat is empty.]

DAILY TASKS
- [task]
- [task]
- [task]

WEEKLY TASKS
- [task]
- [task]

MONTHLY TASKS
- [task]

THE NUMBER
[The single North Star KPI, stated as a number, and how it is reviewed.]

TOOLS YOU WILL USE
[CRM, dialer, spreadsheets, anything else.]

REQUIREMENTS
- [Spoken English level, if the role talks to sellers]
- Reliable internet, [speed] minimum
- [Experience, if genuinely required]

HOW TO APPLY
[What you want them to send, including the screen recording. See below.]
```

## Ask for a screen recording in the application

For any role, ask applicants to send a short screen recording introducing
themselves and showing an internet speed test.

Ninety seconds of that tells you their spoken English, their accent, their
machine, their connection quality, and whether they can follow a simple
instruction. That is more signal than a resume and a first call combined, and it
happens before you have spent any of your own time.

## Post template, prospector

```
Cold Caller / Prospector, Real Estate (Remote, Full Time)

We are a US real estate investment company. We buy houses directly from
owners. You would be the first voice those owners hear.

This is a phone role. The daily expectation is 150 dials minimum and at
least 2 qualified leads passed to our lead manager. Most of our team hits
200. If that number sounds like a problem, this is not the right role and
that is completely fine.

Pay: $[X] per month plus a bonus per qualified lead.
Hours: [hours] US [time zone].

You need:
- Clear, confident spoken English. You will talk to American homeowners
  all day.
- Reliable internet, [speed] minimum.
- The kind of temperament that can hear no forty times and dial again.

Previous cold calling experience helps, but attitude matters more. We
train the script.

To apply, send:
1. A 90 second screen recording introducing yourself, with an internet
   speed test visible.
2. Your resume.

We reply to everyone who does both.
```

```
We are a US real estate investment company. This role keeps our marketing
running on schedule. Nothing we do reaches a homeowner unless this seat
is doing its job.

You would own our property lists: pulling them, cleaning them, uploading

Pay: $[X] per month.

- Care about detail. A wrong tag on a list is a real problem here.
- Reliable internet, [speed] minimum.

You do not need real estate experience. We have written procedures for
every task and we will train you on all of them.

To apply, send:
1. A 90 second screen recording introducing yourself, with an internet
   speed test visible.
2. Your resume.
```

## Writing rules

- Name the number. Always.
- Say what breaks if the seat is empty. It tells people the work matters.
- Do not promise growth, family culture or unlimited opportunity. Everyone
  writes that, so it filters nobody and reads as noise.
- Say you will reply to everyone who applies properly, then do it. Almost
  nobody does, and it is remembered.
- One post per role. A combined listing gets you applicants who fit neither.
