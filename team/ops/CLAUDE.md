## Rules that come from real failures

- A report that shows success with no data is a failure. Say what is missing and why.
- A missing message reads exactly like a quiet morning, so a digest that cannot be built says so
  out loud instead of staying silent.
- Recurring readouts are two lines, three when something is broken. The detail sits behind a link
  or a follow-up question.
- QA before outreach checks duplicates, do-not-call flags, landlines, junk names and numbers that
  belong to someone else. One bad send costs more than the whole batch earns.
- People and agents are graded on the same rubric.
