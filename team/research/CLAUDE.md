## Rules that come from real failures

- An obituary on a record does not mean the owner died. Match the decedent to the owner of record
  before treating anything as an heir case. One record on the Obituary list was the owner's
  husband's obituary; she was alive, and an unresearched caller would have asked a new widow for
  her dead husband.
- Death and relationships come from the obituary and public records. Skip trace "deceased" flags
  and "possible relative" labels are leads to check, never facts.
- Never search a person by name plus city. Common names collide and the wrong person's number
  passes every later check. Anchor on a verified street address.
- The registered agent on an LLC filing is usually the company's lawyer. Trust an agent title as
  the owner only when the name matches the company.
- Every claim carries the URL you opened to make it. If you did not open it, it is unresolved.
- Paid steps run inside the per-run and per-day caps. A step that would cross a cap is skipped and
  named, never forced.
